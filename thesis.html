How this works

No black box

Your sentence is matched against tickers, company names and a handful of themes. Every match is shown, and you can override any weight by hand. Nothing is picked for you in secret.

Every pool, not just one

Each stock trades in several pools on Base at once, and their prices drift apart. The page reads all of them, trades in the deepest, and shows you what the same basket would have cost in the priciest.

Read first, wallet last

Everything above works with no wallet at all. Connect only if you want the whole basket bought in one confirmation — and even then the allowance asked for is exactly the size of that basket, nothing standing.

Only the real tokens

The ten contract addresses here are the ones Coinbase publishes on base.org. Anything else using these tickers on Base was not issued by Coinbase; the page flags pools quoting impossible prices.
