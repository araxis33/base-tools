How this works

No black box

Your sentence is matched against tickers, company names and a handful of themes. Every match is shown, and you can override any weight by hand. Nothing is picked for you in secret.

Every pool, not just one

Each stock trades in several pools on Base at once, and their prices drift apart. The page reads all of them, trades in the deepest, and shows you what the same basket would have cost in the priciest.

Read first, wallet last

Everything above works with no wallet at all, your own holdings included. Connect only to buy a basket or sell what you hold in one confirmation — and even then the allowance asked for is exactly the size of that trade, nothing standing.

Only the real tokens

Every contract address here is one Coinbase publishes on base.org, checked against that list daily. Anything else using these tickers on Base was not issued by Coinbase. A stock whose price on Base strays from the real share is not bought at all.

Not investment advice. The page reads public data and, only if you ask it to, hands your wallet a batch of swaps to confirm; it never holds funds or keys. Coinbase Tokenized Stocks are issued by Coinbase and are only available to eligible users outside the United States — check the official token list and prospectus before you buy anything. Prices, liquidity and price-impact figures are estimates read from public DEX data and can be wrong or stale. Verify the contract address in your own wallet before every trade.
