How it is built. Every night we ask KyberSwap what each of the 36 Coinbase stocks on Base would fetch in USDC at sizes from $100 to $1M, and note the size where the average price is 1% below a small sale. The 10 deepest make the index; each weight is its depth divided by the total of the 10. Measured ….

Why depth. Many stocks on Base have almost nothing in their pools: … of the 36 lose more than 1% on a $50,000 sale and never enter. With depth weights, every stock in the index hits that 1% at the same size of the whole basket, so no single name traps your exit.

On Base is what a $50 buy pays per share right now (KyberSwap routing). Real share is the last trade on the US market, after-hours included. Gap is the difference, adjusted for the token-to-share ratio. Not financial advice.
